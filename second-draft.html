LET’S MAKE IT CLEAR

A few things
you might ask.

Where should I start?+

Think about one part of your day where support would feel welcome. The care planner on this page helps you prepare notes for a conversation.

Can my family help me plan?+

Yes. Invite someone you trust to talk through your preferences with you. Keep your own choices and comfort at the center of that conversation.

What happens on a first visit?+

Start with introductions and a familiar task, such as preparing tea. Leave time afterward to say what worked and what you’d like to change.

How do I ask about prices?+

Agency contact information and pricing are still being prepared for this design preview. You can download your care notes while exploring the concept.

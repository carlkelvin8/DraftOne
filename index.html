OUR SERVICES

Support for real life.

Start with the parts of the day where a little company or practical help would make a difference.

Help around the home

From planning a favorite meal to putting groceries away, share the tasks that keep a household running.

Companionship

A familiar face for a puzzle, a conversation, or a quiet afternoon spent side by side.

Personal care

Respectful help with dressing, moving between rooms, and settling comfortably into an evening routine.

OUR APPROACH

Their home.
Their rhythm.

Begin with what your loved one wants to keep doing for themselves. Then talk together about where a second pair of hands would help.

YOUR FIRST VISIT

Start with one visit.

  1. BEFORE THE VISIT

    Make room for a hello.

    Ask your loved one when they’d enjoy a visitor. An unhurried morning or a quiet afternoon leaves time for introductions.

  2. WHILE YOU’RE TOGETHER

    Do something familiar.

    Put the shopping away together or make a pot of tea. An everyday task gives everyone a chance to learn how your loved one likes things done.

  3. AFTER THE VISIT

    Let them have the last word.

    Ask what felt comfortable, what they’d change, and whether they’d like company again. Use their answers to shape the next visit.

THE FAMILY CHECK-IN

“What felt easier today?”

Some weeks it might be getting lunch ready. Other weeks it might be having someone to talk to. Make time to ask again as routines change.
